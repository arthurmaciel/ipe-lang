-- BARE `_` AS A WHOLE `LET` BINDING PATTERN IS NOT ALLOWED - test.ipe

a bare `_` cannot be the whole binding pattern in a `let`.

 --> test.ipe:4:9
  |
4 |     let _ = Io.println "a" in
  |         ^ `_` as the whole binding pattern binds nothing — use a `do` line or `|> Task.andThen (\_ -> …)` to sequence an effect
  |
