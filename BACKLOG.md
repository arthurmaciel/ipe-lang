> phases used in this table. Read on demand; update as items land.

> **Progressive-development loop contract:** rows whose **Notes** column
> contains the literal tag `[progdev-safe]` are loop-eligible (mechanical,
> reference-backed, no design decision). The loop removes a row when it lands
> the item, or updates the row's Notes to record the new blocker.

> with a workaround (root-cause fixes only; no-deferral). Priorities:
